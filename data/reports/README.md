## Folder Structure

- **`proposal/`** — Original project proposal, research objectives, and planned methodology.
- **`final-report/`** — Final research paper documenting methodology, experiments, results, and conclusions.
- **`presentation/`** — Presentation materials summarizing the project and key findings.

## Documentation Guidelines

- Ensure consistency between the proposal, experimental results, final report, and presentation.
- Base conclusions on validated experimental findings from `../results/`.
- Document methodology, assumptions, limitations, and references.
- Maintain final versions of deliverables in their respective folders.
